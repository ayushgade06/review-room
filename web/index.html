    New to cricket?

    Four ideas, and you can follow everything here.

    1. A wicket is an out. Each team bats once: 120 balls or 10 outs. Losing a batter hurts, like an out with the bases loaded.
    2. The umpire calls it. "Out" or "not out", on the spot. The most disputed calls are LBW: the ball hit the leg but would have hit the stumps.
    3. Either team can challenge. That's DRS, cricket's replay review. Same idea as the NFL red flag or a tennis Hawk-Eye challenge.
    4. Challenges are limited. Two failed ones per innings. If the replay is too close to call ("umpire's call"), the team keeps its challenge.

    Every challenge is a bet: risk a challenge you might need later, to fix a call that might cost you the game. We measure both sides in win chance, like a TV win-probability line.
