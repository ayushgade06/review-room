Method & limits

The bet, in one line

Challenge if your confidence × what the call is worth is bigger than your doubt × what a lost challenge costs.

Rearranged, the minimum confidence is p* = (1 − u)L / (G + (1 − u)L), where G is what the call is worth, L what a failed challenge costs, and u the share of failures that are "umpire's call" (challenge kept).

How the pieces are measured

What a call is worth: the drop in win chance from losing that batter, from a model trained on 8,897 matches — think of a TV win-probability line. What a failed challenge costs: how much win chance teams typically gain later from the challenges they still hold, measured from real matches. Challenges left: rebuilt from the order of challenges in each innings; it matches the IPL's 2022 rule change from one to two.

What this does not claim

We cannot see calls nobody challenged, and we have no ball-tracking data, so the player's own read of the ball is the missing ingredient — the tool asks for it rather than guessing it. All numbers are estimates, and patterns in behaviour are not proof of cause and effect.
